Methodology & limitations

Each group is a GDELT DOC 2.0 keyword query. Groups are applied in priority order and each one excludes the keywords of the groups before it, so every article falls in exactly one group. An article mentioning both negotiations and missiles counts as diplomacy. "Everything else" is not queried: it is derived as total − groups, for both volume and volume‑weighted tone.

The decomposition compares the selected timeframe with the preceding one of equal length (War Total: first 30 days vs last 30 days) using volume‑weighted averages, so its totals can differ slightly from the simple daily mean in the chart above. It is exact: tone effect + mix effect = total change.

Limitations. GDELT scores the tone of the whole article, so a piece that mixes the war with other topics cannot be split. Keyword groups depend on the chosen words: an unlucky keyword can pull unrelated stories into a group. Days where any series is missing are left out of the group charts.
